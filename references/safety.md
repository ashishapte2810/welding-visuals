# Safety and scope language

- Figures explain **mechanisms**. They never give settings, procedures, or "how to weld" steps. Do not turn parameter lists into advice.
- Every page carries: "For education only. This is not a welding procedure specification or a substitute for your WPS, applicable code, training or workplace safety rules, including eye, skin and respiratory protection."
- Do not claim acceptance, qualification or code compliance. Refer to the governing code and the user's procedure.
- Toggles that show a defect (expulsion, inclusion) explain the cause and effect only; no instruction on how to cause or avoid it beyond what a cited source states, in the source's words.
- Name a safety standard (for example ANSI Z49.1, **memory; verify**) in the footer only after it has been checked.
- If a user asks for welding parameters or procedure steps, point to the governing code and their qualified procedure, offer the mechanism explanation, and use values only from documents they provide, with edition and page.

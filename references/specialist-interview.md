# Audience brief and expert interview

Ask these once per skill (and again per new process).

**Audience**: Who will use the figures (apprentices, technicians, engineers, inspectors)? Typical prior knowledge? Language and terminology to prefer? Units (SI or US customary)? Where will it be shown (meeting, training portal, print, phone)?

**Scope**: Which processes first? Which materials and thicknesses are the base case? Which governing codes and customer standards apply? What must never be implied?

**Content**: What do learners most often get wrong? Which three defects cost the most? Which parameters would they want to change with a slider, and what are realistic ranges in your shop (from which document)? Which photographs or macros are the ground truth for proportions?

**Review**: Who verifies claims, how quickly, and in what form (checklist, call, markup)? What counts as "verified"? Who owns the document library and its confidentiality?

**Delivery**: HTML file, link, video, slide, PDF? Light, white or dark? Branding?

Record the answers in `references/library/INDEX.md` (documents) and the relevant `processes/*.md` file (decisions).

## Terms formally defined in the 7th ed. vol. 1 glossary (1976)
- **nugget (resistance welding):** the weld metal joining the parts in spot, roll spot, seam or projection welds (p.307). **nugget size:** diameter or width measured in the plane of the interface between the pieces (p.307).
- **off time (resistance welding):** the time during which the electrodes are off the work; generally used when the welding cycle is repetitive (p.307). **temper time** is part of the post-weld interval before hold time (p.319).
- **faying surface:** the mating surface of a member in contact or close proximity with another member to which it is to be joined (p.296).
- **resistance spot welding (RSW):** a resistance welding process producing coalescence at the faying surfaces in one spot, by the heat of resistance to current through the work parts held together under force by electrodes (p.313; the definition continues beyond what was read).
